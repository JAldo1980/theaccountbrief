I've spent more than 12 years in B2B marketing, SEO and agency account management. In that time I've written strategies, run client meetings, co-founded an agency (Lead Kinetics) and built websites. The Account Brief is where I'm going to write up what I've learned.

## The gap I want to fill

Most B2B marketing advice comes from vendors selling a tool or in-house teams with one company to think about. Much less comes from the account side: the person who has to turn a strategy into results a client will renew for.

## What I'll write about

- **B2B strategy:** frameworks, budgets and KPIs that hold up in front of a board.

- **Client services:** onboarding, reporting, retention and the difficult conversations.
